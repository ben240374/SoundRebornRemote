SoundReborn Remote

En français

Android remote for Bose SoundTouch speakers. Everything happens on your local network: no account, no cloud, no service in between.

Download the APK Source code

The STR agent must be on the speaker. It is what gives SoundTouch speakers back what the Bose cloud provided until it shut down on 6 May 2026. You install it with the STR tool for PC or Mac — st-reborn.de. This application does not install it; it flags the speakers that do not have it.

What the application controls

  • Volume, mute, standby, bass
  • Play, pause, next and previous track
  • The six presets, with station logos
  • Internet station search, and assigning a station to a key
  • Speaker sources, Bluetooth, AUX, playing an arbitrary stream
  • Multi-room groups: whole-group volume, per-speaker volume, levelling in one key
  • Commands follow the group's master, because the master is what plays
  • Speaker discovery over mDNS, falling back to a network sweep
  • Interface in English, French, German, Dutch and Spanish, light or dark theme

Thanks to STR

When Bose shut down its cloud, SoundTouch speakers lost most of what made them useful. STR — SoundTouch Reborn, by Jens Roggenfelder (JRpersonal), is the agent that runs on the speaker itself and gives back what the cloud provided. This application does nothing more than talk to it: without his work it would have nothing to control.
